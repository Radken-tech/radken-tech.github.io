How it works

You approve. The system does the rest.

We start by understanding your business: what you sell, to whom, and at what price. Then the system works every weekday.

Finds

Each morning it finds new organisations that match your ideal client, with their published phone and email.

Assesses

It works out which of your services each one is likely to need, and ranks them by priority.

Drafts

It writes a short, tailored email to each one in your name, plus a follow-up after five days.

You approve

You read, edit and approve. Emails go out from your own address.

Questions

Common questions

Does the system send emails by itself?

No. It only prepares drafts. You read, edit and approve every message before it goes out.

Where do the prospects come from?

From business websites, official directories and public listings. We only record phone numbers and emails the businesses have published themselves.

Do I need to be technical?

No. If you can use email and WhatsApp on your phone, you can use this system.

How long until it's running?

Usually within a week of our first session.
